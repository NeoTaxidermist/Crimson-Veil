On 2026-11-19 the user agreed firmly: no stock characters and no stock environment sets — they would not fit the ink-silhouette style and would blur the game's concept. All models stay script-built (ArtSource/*_build.py) and procedural. The last fully self-made state is tagged `withoutAssets` (= v0.32.0 / alpha_8, commit 6b22345).

**Why:** the look is a custom ink silhouette after the user's own concept art.

**How to apply:** never propose asset-store characters or environment kits. External things still open to discuss: motion-capture animation (e.g. Mixamo, retargeted onto our rig), CC0/CC-BY sound and music, Cinemachine for cutscenes, possibly a CC0 base mesh for the face only. Ask before any download; the user signs in to services themself. See [[crimson-veil-project]], [[crimson-veil-cutscenes]].
